Feedback

Documentation home

Your feedback decides what changes in the next versions. Tell us what works, what does not work and what you need.

How to send feedback

MethodHow
In the plannerSelect Help, Send feedback, or Send feedback on the start screen. Fill in the type, the area, the usefulness, a summary and the details. Open the feedback form opens the feedback form of the project in a new browser tab with your text filled in: you check it and submit it there. The planner itself sends nothing.
On the project pageOpen the Issues page of the project, select New issue, then Feedback (ideas, questions, praise) or Problem (something does not work).
With no internet accessIn the planner feedback window, select Copy as text and send the text by e-mail to the person who gave you the package.
No customer data. Do not put host names, IP addresses, VLAN IDs, inventory files, project files or credentials in the feedback. Use made-up values, for example esx01.example.local and 10.0.0.0/24, to show a problem.

Good feedback

  • One topic in each item.
  • For a problem: what you did, what you expected, what happened, the version (Help, About) and the browser.
  • For an idea: the task that you want to do and why it is difficult now.
  • The usefulness score (1 to 5) helps us to see the trend from version to version.

What happens with your feedback

StepWhat we do
ReceivedEach item gets the label feedback or problem automatically.
Triage (each week)We read each new item, ask questions if necessary, and add an area label and a priority: priority: high (stops the work, or a wrong change is possible), priority: medium, priority: low. Duplicates are closed with a link to the first item.
PlannedItems for the next version get the milestone of that version. Items that we do not plan get a short reason.
ReleasedThe change log of the version lists the items with their numbers. The item is closed with the version that has the change.

The maintainers review the open feedback before each version and record the decisions in the items.
